The Edge

Not another chart scanner. A wallet-convergence engine.

Most tools watch price. ALPHAHOUND watches who is buying. Every runner on this chain was accumulated early by the same recurring cast of wallets — we mapped all of them on-chain and made their agreement a number you can read at a glance.

01 · CONVERGENCE

Ambush Convergence

For any token, we pull its recent on-chain buyers and intersect them with 7,819 wallets that historically bought golden dogs early. The count is the signal: ≥10 is strong, ≥25 is golden-dog grade.

02 · TOP HUNTER

Top-Hunter Override

Four wallets have caught more $1M runners than anyone else. When one of them steps into a token — even a tiny 40-buyer pool — that alone flags it, before the crowd math can catch up.

small pools scored by rate, not raw count
03 · IGNITION

Ignition Detection

Pumps show up on-chain as buy-volume bursts. We bucket the pool by block and flag the moment recent buying spikes past 3× its own average — read from the same data, no extra RPC.

runners ignite every ~1–4 min while pumping
04 · MAKERS

Market-Maker Map

43 verified EOA market makers seed liquidity across multiple runners before the crowd arrives. Contracts and routers get filtered out first — infrastructure is not a signal.
